A licensed pop-up is not a queue-management problem. It is a credibility test, and the fans are the inspectors.

When MINISO opened its first official MARVEL store in Hong Kong, the crowd was not there to browse. They came to check: is this real, is the print sharp, does the packaging match, is the price honest. That audience forgives a lot. It does not forgive fake.

So the design brief was not "make it look Marvel". It was "make it survive being examined". Detail at eye level, not just above the door. Nothing printed that a fan could photograph and laugh at.

Most activation budgets go to whatever photographs well from the entrance. Fans spend their time looking at the thing in their hand.

I do not believe footfall is the metric of an IP event. The metric is whether the fan posts a picture of the product, or a picture of the sign.

If your next licensed event had thirty percent fewer people and every one of them photographed the product, would that be a worse outcome?

授權快閃店唔係人流管理問題，係一場 credibility 測試——而粉絲就係驗貨員。

MINISO 開香港第一間 MARVEL 官方授權主題店嗰次，嚟嘅人唔係路過逛街。佢哋係嚟驗：係唔係真、印刷夠唔夠利、包裝對唔對、價錢誠唔誠實。呢班觀眾好寬容，但佢哋唔寬容假。以前資訊唔對稱，品牌講咩就係咩；而家粉絲上網五分鐘就對到全球版本，你呃唔到。

所以設計簡報唔係「做到似 Marvel」，係「做到經得起審」。細節要做到眼平高度，唔係只做入口位——入口位係影相用，眼平位係驗貨用。任何一樣嘢，如果粉絲舉機一影就會笑，就唔可以出。連排隊動線我哋都當內容設計：條龍要行過產品牆，唔係行過一幅背板。

大部分品牌活動預算，都花喺「由入口影過去靚」嘅地方。但粉絲嘅時間，係花喺「手上面嗰件嘢」。

我唔相信人流係 IP 活動嘅成績表。真正嘅成績表係：粉絲影嘅係產品，定係個招牌。

Putting a celebrity in your store buys attention for one afternoon. Giving a customer the celebrity's job buys something else.

For Levi's Eyewear we did not do an endorsement. We let selected customers run the shop for a day: take the role, serve real walk-ins, make the small decisions a shop manager makes. The celebrity was the frame, not the content.

The difference matters. An endorsement asks you to admire someone. A role asks you to imagine yourself. Only one of those leaves something behind after the photos are taken.

What we were really building was permission. Permission to touch, to try, to be in the picture instead of watching it.

I keep seeing activation budgets spent on how a stage looks, and almost nothing spent on what a visitor is allowed to do. The memory lives in the second one.

Think about your last event. What were visitors allowed to do that they could not do in a shop on a normal Tuesday?

請名人落舖，買到一個下午嘅注意力。畀客人做名人嗰份工，買到另一樣嘢。

Levi's Eyewear 嗰次唔係做代言。我哋安排客人做一日店長——真正接客、真正開單、真正應付行入嚟嘅人、真正做錯決定然後補救。名人只係一個框架，唔係內容。

分野好重要。代言係叫你欣賞一個人；角色係叫你想像自己。只有後者，喺影完相之後仲有嘢留低。而家嘅消費者對「名人站台」已經免疫——佢哋一眼睇得出邊個係收錢講嘢。同一個名人，如果佢變成你同事，說服力完全唔同。

我哋實際砌嘅係一種許可：許你摸、許你試、許你入鏡、許你講錯嘢，而唔係企喺外面睇。沉浸唔係場景做得多靚，係你能唔能夠喺入面做一件真嘅事。

分別就係咁簡單，但好多活動都做唔到——因為請名人比設計一個角色容易。

呢種設計有一個副作用：客人會唔捨得走，而唔捨得走嘅人，先會買嘢。所以我們唔係做一場活動，係做一個職位出嚟。

Government events never lack spec. They lack one sentence people remember.

I have sat through a lot of them. The shape is always the same: speeches, a photo, four technical presentations, twenty minutes each, thirty words a slide. Three hundred industry people walk out of the convention centre and not one of them can tell you the point.

For the Buildings Department's Building Safety Seminar 2025 the theme was digitalisation: BIM, MiC, smart site safety, AI inspection. All four are real and all four matter. But give four things equal weight and you have said nothing.

What we changed was not the spec. It was sequence and emphasis. Each session kept one action an attendee could use on Monday morning, and everything else went to the appendix. The stage explained the reason, not the full detail. Even the walking route and where the exhibits sat were arranged around how many seconds a person would stop, not around an organisation chart.

I do not believe complexity signals rigour. Real professionalism is doing the homework for your audience: which sentence must they remember, and what can they read themselves.

After your next event, ask three people in the room which line stuck. If you get three different answers, where do you think the problem is?

政府活動最唔缺嘅係規格。最缺嘅係一句人記得嘅重點。

我坐過唔少。流程永遠一樣：致辭、合照、四場技術簡報、每場二十分鐘、每張 slide 三十個字、場刊廿頁。做完之後三百個業界人士行出會展，你問佢今日邊一句最記得，佢會望住你。

屋宇署樓宇安全研討會 2025，主題係數碼轉型——BIM、MiC、安全智慧工地、AI 勘察。四樣都係真、四樣都重要。但四樣平均用力，等於四樣都冇講。與其證明我哋咩都識，不如令台下真係帶走一樣。

所以我哋改嘅唔係規格，係次序同重量：每一節只留一個「星期一返到公司可以用」嘅動作，其餘全部收去附件。台上講原因——點解要轉、唔轉會點——唔講全部細節。連會場動線同展品擺位，都係跟「人會停幾秒」去排，唔係跟機構架構圖去排。

規格係入場券，唔係內容。規格做唔好會出事，但規格做得好，冇人記得。

我唔相信「複雜＝認真」。真正嘅專業，係替觀眾做功課：哪一句必須記得，哪啲佢自己睇得明。呢個唔叫簡化，呢個叫尊重。

你下一個活動做完，問台下三個人「今日邊句最記得」。如果三個答案都唔同，你覺得問題出喺邊？
