Questions you may have

I am not 65 yet. Is it too early to plan?

The planning window is 50–65: MPF can be withdrawn from 60, and the expected retirement age is about 62.

Will moving to the Greater Bay Area affect my Hong Kong benefits?

The Guangdong Scheme and the residential care scheme are cross-border arrangements, each with its own eligibility rules, such as one year of residence in Hong Kong.

Can I use the Elderly Health Care Voucher there?

Yes. The pilot covers 21 service points across nine mainland GBA cities.

How long is the wait for a care home place?

Supply is not evenly distributed: six of the 29 homes are stretched, while occupancy in some districts is very low.

Do you take commission?

We do not collect payments for care homes or property. See the risk disclosure page for how we charge.
