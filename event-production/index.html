Firebean designs, builds and operates events across Hong Kong: mall roadshows and pop-ups, product launches and ceremonies, and roving exhibitions that tour venue to venue — one team from concept to pack-down.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle mall roadshows and pop-ups in Hong Kong?
Yes — atrium builds, sampling counters and short-run pop-ups, including venue liaison, staffing and daily operations.
Can one exhibition tour multiple venues?
Yes. Firebean builds modular exhibitions engineered for repeated transport and re-installation; the roving programme has covered 32 projects for 5 government bureaus since 2016.
How many venues can a campaign cover?
It depends on the build and the schedule, but multi-venue tours across Hong Kong Island, Kowloon and the New Territories are routine for us. We plan the route with you before we design the booth.
Do you provide interactive technology?
Yes — touchscreen kiosks, simple games and data-capture experiences that let us report real participation numbers afterwards.
Who handles permits and venue rules?
We do. Venue liaison, floor plans, insurance and on-site compliance are part of production, not an extra.
How early should we brief you?
Four to eight weeks for a standard activation; longer for a custom build or a multi-venue tour, because fabrication and venue approvals drive the calendar.

Talk to us

Email hello@firebean.net or call the Wan Chai studio. Tell us the audience you need to reach and the dates — we will come back with a scope, not a sales deck.
