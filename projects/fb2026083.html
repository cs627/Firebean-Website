English

Rethinking Civic Education: Moving Beyond Static Displays to Immersive Engagement

Firebean demonstrates how transmuting constitutional education into kinetic retail experiences creates durable civic affinity.

For decades, conventional civic outreach has adhered to a rigid, top-down paradigm: informational leaflets, text-heavy exhibition panels, and didactic monologues. While well-intentioned, these traditional formats frequently fail to capture the attention of modern families within hyper-stimulating public spaces. In conceptualizing the roving workshop series for the Constitutional and Mainland Affairs Bureau, Firebean rejected the standard bureaucratic convention. Rather than treating statutory legal frameworks as a lecture, the strategic imperative was to transform complex legal principles into an intuitive, tactile experience that seamlessly integrates into the weekend routines of parents and children across major commercial centers.

The creative strategy centered on the metaphor of a public transit network. The Constitution and the Basic Law serve as the societal roadmap and stabilizing bedrock of Hong Kong, providing structural guidance much like a meticulously planned transit artery. To materialize this concept, Firebean engineered the 'Owl Bus Stop' narrative, introducing an approachable owl IP alongside a life-sized, walk-in bus pavilion. Crucially, this centerpiece was not merely a passive photographic installation. It served a dual operational purpose, elegantly concealing backstage logistics, material storage, and a check-in reception desk within its structural facade, thereby maintaining total aesthetic immersion within high-traffic shopping mall atriums.

Moving far beyond generic promotional collateral, the activation deployed five interactive zones engineered around hands-on creation and gamified learning. Participants designed bespoke heat-shrink keychains, customized bus-themed 'itabags' with curated graphic badges, folded spray-tinted paper buses, and tackled didactic Q&A matching modules. A standout attraction, the mini-depot rolling wheel installation, challenged younger children through physical mechanics designed specifically around pediatric ergonomics. Each activity subtly anchored constitutional milestones within achievable micro-tasks, ensuring that abstract legal concepts were absorbed organically through personal accomplishment.

From an operational and PR perspective, this multi-mall tour across venues such as tmtplaza, SPOT, and MOSTown proved that public education can generate authentic commercial synergy. By transforming open retail atriums into participatory zones of discovery, the project sustained balanced footfall, eliminated congestive bottlenecks through disciplined queuing design, and produced take-home artifacts that extended message retention long after the event concluded. Firebean provided a comprehensive scope of work—spanning initial branding and IP modeling to UI/UX, video production, and turnkey on-site management—demonstrating how thoughtful spatial design can completely redefine public-sector engagement.

Genuine civic engagement is never achieved through passive reading, but through deliberate, memorable human participation.

顛覆常規公營策劃：法治推廣如何告別單向說教

Firebean 將法制教育融入沉浸式商場互動體驗，開創公眾參與及政策傳播新維度。

長期以來，公營機構的法制推廣往往難以擺脫傳統思維框架：密集的單張派發、靜態的展板陳列以及單向的宣導演說。這類刻板模式在節奏明快、視覺衝擊豐富的現代商業空間中，極易被市民邊緣化。Firebean 團隊在為政制及內地事務局構思巡迴工作坊時，毅然挑戰這種既定常規。我們認為，深奧嚴肅的法制條文不應停留於紙面理論，而應轉化為親切易懂、具備強烈互動意願的實體體驗，自然融入家庭受眾的日常生活場景之中。

本次策劃以「貓頭鷹巴士站」為核心意象，將《憲法》與《基本法》比喻為香港行穩致遠的「清晰路線圖」與「堅實基石」，精確呼應巴士站網絡為城市規劃繁榮路線的功能。Firebean 團隊在屯門市廣場、上水廣場及新港城中心等頂級商場中庭，打造出極具視覺張力的大型主題巴士裝置與親民貓頭鷹 IP。這座主裝置絕非僅供拍照的道具，其內部結構精妙融合了接待服務台與後勤物資儲藏空間，在確保商場高密度人流順暢運轉的同時，維持現場全景沉浸的美學完整度。

為徹底打破「政策推廣缺乏趣味」的痛點，團隊捨棄了敷衍的填色活動，策劃出五大兼具精緻度與操作性的主題體驗。現場包括專屬手繪熱縮膠鎖匙扣、結合潮流元素的立體拼貼巴士「痛袋」、噴色立體摺紙巴士以及互動問答配對展板。針對幼童群體特別研發的「車輪滾動吧」迷你維修廠裝置，更以符合兒童人體工學的坡度與軌道設計，讓孩童在反覆推動車輪完成挑戰的過程中，潛移默化地吸收法治秩序的運作邏輯。

從公共關係與體驗策略的角度審視，本次巡迴活動成功為公營機構建立了「軟性浸潤式」教育的示範標竿。全方位的服務範疇——涵蓋論述構思、品牌策略、IP 視覺體系、UI/UX 設計、短片製作以至現場全面運營與技術支援——展現了 Firebean 在處理高難度公營項目時的綜合交付實力。參與者親手製作的精美紀念品具備長期留存價值，讓原本抽象的法律概念在家庭對話中持續發酵，達成品牌效益、公民教育與商場客流的三贏局面。

深度的政策傳播從不依賴強制灌輸，唯有藉由動手共創的真實體驗，方能鑄就持久的社會認同。
