Transforming Public Safety Education: Firebean's Innovative Approach to the Buildings Department's Safety Carnival

In a city as dynamic as Hong Kong, ensuring public awareness of building safety is paramount. However, the technical nature of building regulations often presents a significant communication challenge. The Buildings Department approached Firebean with a clear goal: to educate the public about crucial building safety issues, but with a critical pain point – how to make this vital information engaging, accessible, and memorable for diverse demographics, moving beyond traditional, often dry, informational campaigns. They needed a strategic partner to foster proactive maintenance awareness and participation.

The Challenge: Demystifying Building Safety for All

The core problem Firebean identified was the gap between complex governmental information and public engagement. Traditional outreach methods, while informative, often struggled to capture sustained attention, especially among families and younger generations. The task was not merely to disseminate facts, but to instigate a behavioral shift towards proactive building maintenance and hazard identification. This required a fresh, innovative approach to public education.

Firebean's Strategic Blueprint: Gamification as the Core

Firebean's analytical problem-solving methodology led to a transformative solution: a 'Building Safety Carnival.' Our strategic blueprint centered on gamification – converting abstract safety concepts into tangible, interactive, and enjoyable experiences. We meticulously developed three bespoke game booths, each designed to address specific building safety issues in an engaging format:

  • '1-2-3, Red Light, Green Light, Can't be at ease without checking buildings': This twist on a classic game taught participants the swift identification and correct handling of common defects like pipe damage, faulty windows, and unauthorized structures, emphasizing quick and accurate responses.
  • 'Dangerous Building Sprite Sharpshooter': Participants used toy guns to 'shoot down' problematic 'sprites' representing various building hazards, turning the fight against safety risks into a fun, interactive challenge.
  • 'Left 'Building', Right 'Building' Maze': A labyrinth where participants navigated by answering questions on building maintenance, blending cognitive learning with motor skills development.

Beyond concept, our PR consulting and social media strategy built anticipation, leveraging local mascots and tailored content to resonate deeply with the Hong Kong public.

From Concept to Carnival: Meticulous Execution by Firebean

With the strategic framework in place, Firebean moved into comprehensive event planning and production. Our team managed every facet, from securing prime, high-traffic venues like Tuen Mun Town Plaza and Olympian City 2 to the intricate design and fabrication of each interactive booth. Ensuring accessibility, safety, and a seamless flow for thousands of visitors over multiple weekends was paramount. On-site, Firebean's operational expertise shone through. Our dedicated team meticulously managed crowd control, booth operations, staff training, and real-time troubleshooting, guaranteeing a smooth, positive, and educational experience for every attendee.

Tangible Impact: Building a Safer Community Together

The 'Building Safety Carnival' was an resounding success, attracting thousands of participants who actively engaged with the safety messages. Firebean's innovative approach successfully transformed a potentially dry subject into a vibrant community event, significantly boosting public awareness and fostering a proactive culture towards building safety. The project not only met but exceeded the client's expectations, demonstrating the power of strategic ingenuity combined with flawless execution in public education.

Through strategic innovation and flawless execution, Firebean successfully transformed complex building safety information into an engaging, accessible, and impactful public education carnival, setting a new benchmark for community outreach.

革新樓宇安全教育：Firebean為屋宇署締造互動嘉年華的成功案例

在香港這個急速發展的城市，提升公眾對樓宇安全的意識至關重要。然而，樓宇法規的專業性和技術性往往成為溝通上的挑戰。屋宇署找到Firebean，目標明確：期望能以一種引人入勝、易於理解且令人難忘的方式，向廣大市民普及重要的樓宇安全知識，打破傳統宣傳活動的沉悶感。他們需要一個策略夥伴，能有效促進市民主動關注樓宇保養。

挑戰：如何將複雜樓宇安全知識普及化？

Firebean深入分析，發現核心問題在於政府部門複雜資訊與市民參與度之間的巨大鴻溝。傳統的宣傳方式雖然資訊豐富，但難以持續吸引公眾，特別是家庭和小朋友。我們的任務不僅是傳遞事實，更是要激發市民主動維護樓宇和識別危險的行為轉變。這要求一種嶄新、創意的公眾教育模式。

Firebean的策略藍圖：以遊戲化開創互動學習新體驗

Firebean憑藉其「分析問題解決」的方法論，提出了一個革命性的解決方案：「樓宇安全嘉年華」。我們的策略藍圖以「遊戲化」為核心，將抽象的樓宇安全概念轉化為具體、互動且充滿樂趣的體驗。我們精心設計了三個獨特的遊戲攤位，每個都旨在以引人入勝的方式解決特定的樓宇安全問題：

  • 「一二三，紅綠燈，唔Check樓，點放心」：這個經典遊戲的變奏版，教導參加者迅速識別並正確處理常見的樓宇失修問題，例如渠管破損、窗戶欠妥和僭建，強調快速而準確的反應。
  • 「危樓精靈神槍手」：參加者利用玩具槍「擊落」代表不同樓宇危險的「精靈」，將打擊安全風險的行動變成一個有趣、互動的挑戰。
  • 向左「樓」，向右「樓」迷宮：一個迷宮遊戲，參加者需回答樓宇維修保養問題才能找到出路，將認知學習與手眼協調訓練結合。

除了概念設計，我們的公關諮詢和社交媒體策略也為嘉年華建立了熱烈期待，利用本地吉祥物和量身定制的內容，深入引起香港市民的共鳴。

從概念到盛會：Firebean的精密策劃與執行

在策略框架確定後，Firebean展開了全面的活動策劃與製作。我們的團隊負責每一個環節，從確保屯門市廣場和奧海城二期等主要、人流密集的活動場地，到每個互動攤位的精細設計和搭建。確保活動對數以萬計的訪客在多個週末都能暢通無阻、安全且流暢地進行至關重要。在現場，Firebean卓越的運營能力體現得淋漓盡致。我們的專責團隊細緻管理人群流動、攤位運作、員工培訓和即時故障排除，確保每位參與者都能獲得順暢、積極且具教育意義的體驗。

成果斐然：共建安全社區的影響力

「樓宇安全嘉年華」取得了巨大的成功，吸引了數以萬計的市民積極參與樓宇安全資訊。Firebean的創新方法成功將一個可能枯燥的議題轉變為充滿活力的社區盛會，顯著提升了公眾意識，並培養了主動關注樓宇安全的文化。該項目不僅達到，甚至超越了客戶的預期，展示了策略智慧與完美執行在公眾教育方面的巨大影響力。
