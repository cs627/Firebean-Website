Firebean Case Study: Igniting Youth Engagement for Nestle Drumstick with Street Music

The Challenge: Connecting Nestle Drumstick with a New Generation

Nestle Drumstick faced a critical challenge: launching a new ice cream bar and establishing a profound, authentic connection with Hong Kong's elusive youth demographic. Traditional marketing tactics were proving insufficient to cut through the noise of a saturated market. The client needed an innovative approach that not only drove product trial but also built a lasting brand perception as a supporter of local culture and youth initiatives. Their pain point was clear: how to transcend conventional advertising and create a truly experiential campaign that resonated emotionally with young consumers.

Firebean's Strategic Solution: Harnessing the Power of Street Culture

At Firebean, we recognized that to genuinely engage youth, we needed to meet them where they were and speak their language. Our strategic analysis led us to a groundbreaking concept: the 'Street Music Performance X Drumstick' campaign. We developed a holistic strategy focused on integrating the product launch within the vibrant fabric of Hong Kong's street music scene. Firebean took the lead in conceptualizing the entire event, developing a branding strategy that fused Drumstick's playful image with the raw energy of street art and music. Our solution was multifaceted. Firebean identified prime youth-centric locations, particularly the bustling Tsim Sha Tsui Ferry Pier, as ideal stages. We meticulously curated a diverse lineup of popular local street musicians, transforming their performances into interactive brand touchpoints. A core element of Firebean's strategy was a seamless integration of digital engagement: attendees could scan a QR code to follow Nestle's social media platforms (Instagram and Facebook) and immediately receive a complimentary new Drumstick bar. This mechanism provided instant gratification while delivering measurable social media growth for the client.

Flawless Execution: Amplifying Reach Through Authentic Voices

Firebean’s comprehensive scope of work ensured flawless execution from start to finish. Our team managed every detail, from visual identity development that captured the campaign's edgy vibe to meticulous event planning and on-site operation. Crucially, Firebean's expertise in influencer seeding played a pivotal role. We didn't just invite musicians; we empowered them to become authentic brand advocates. Each performer delivered a unique '1-minute shout out,' publicly thanking Nestle Drumstick for their support, a gesture that resonated deeply with their fan bases and amplified the brand's message with unparalleled authenticity. By strategically leveraging the musicians' existing media channels and fan loyalty, Firebean dramatically extended the campaign's reach beyond the physical event, tapping into a vast network of music lovers. This approach not only introduced the new ice cream bar to thousands but also successfully reshaped Drumstick's brand image, positioning it as a dynamic supporter of youth culture, local talent, and the spirit of Hong Kong. The campaign successfully cemented Drumstick as the 'must-eat' dessert for young people, aligning the brand with positivity, creativity, and community. Through Firebean's innovative experiential strategy, Nestle Drumstick didn't just launch a product; it launched a movement, embedding itself firmly within the heart of Hong Kong's youth culture.

Firebean 案例研究：以街頭音樂點燃雀巢Drumstick的青年社群

挑戰：雀巢Drumstick 如何與新一代消費者建立連結？

雀巢Drumstick 面臨一項重大挑戰：如何在推出全新雪糕批時，與香港的年輕族群建立真正而深入的連結。傳統的行銷手法已難以在飽和的市場中脫穎而出。客戶需要一種創新策略，不僅能促進產品試用，更能建立一種支持本地文化和青年活動的品牌形象。他們的核心痛點很明確：如何超越傳統廣告，打造一個能夠在情感上引起年輕消費者共鳴的體驗式活動。

Firebean 的策略性解決方案：駕馭街頭文化的能量

Firebean 深知，要真正吸引年輕人，就必須走到他們身邊，用他們的語言溝通。經過策略分析，我們提出了突破性的概念：「街頭音樂表演X Drumstick」活動。我們制定了一項全面的策略，將產品發布會融入香港充滿活力的街頭音樂場景中。Firebean 主導了整個活動的概念發展，設計了將Drumstick 的玩味形象與街頭藝術和音樂的原始能量相結合的品牌策略。 我們的解決方案是多方面的。Firebean 鎖定了主要的年輕人聚集地，特別是熙來攘往的尖沙咀碼頭，作為理想的表演舞台。我們精心策劃了多位受歡迎的本地街頭音樂表演者陣容，將他們的表演轉化為互動式的品牌接觸點。Firebean 策略的核心要素是無縫整合數位互動：參與者可以掃描QR code 追蹤雀巢的社交媒體平台（Instagram 和Facebook），並立即獲得一支免費的全新Drumstick 雪糕批。這個機制不僅提供了即時的滿足感，也為客戶帶來了可衡量的社交媒體增長。

完美執行：透過真實的聲音擴大影響力

Firebean 廣泛的工作範圍確保了從概念到執行的完美無瑕。我們的團隊管理每一個細節，從捕捉活動前衛氛圍的視覺識別設計，到精密的活動規劃和現場營運。至關重要的是，Firebean 在影響者播種方面的專業知識發揮了關鍵作用。我們不僅邀請了音樂家，更讓他們成為真正的品牌倡導者。每位表演者都進行了獨特的「1分鐘shout out」，公開感謝雀巢Drumstick 的全力支持，這個舉動在他們的粉絲群中引起了深刻共鳴，以無與倫比的真實性擴大了品牌的訊息。 透過策略性地利用音樂家自身的媒體管道和粉絲忠誠度，Firebean 大幅擴展了活動的影響力，超越了實體活動本身，觸及了廣大的音樂愛好者網絡。這種方法不僅向數千人介紹了新的雪糕批，更成功地重塑了Drumstick 的品牌形象，將其定位為支持青年文化、本地人才和香港精神的活力品牌。該活動成功地將Drumstick 鞏固為年輕人「必吃」的甜品，使品牌與積極、創意和社群聯繫在一起。 透過 Firebean 創新的體驗式策略，雀巢Drumstick 不僅僅是推出了一款產品；它更發起了一場運動，將自身牢固地植根於香港青年文化的核心。

FAQ

How did Firebean address Nestle Drumstick's challenge of authentically connecting with the youth demographic in Hong Kong?

Beyond event attendees, how did Firebean extend the reach and impact of the Nestle Drumstick street music campaign?
