Disrupting Civic Education: Transforming Legal Statutes into a Sci-Fi Arena

How Firebean dismantled the legacy of didactic policy exhibitions by deploying high-immersion robotics and gamified experiential design across Hong Kong's major retail hubs.

Public policy roadshows have long suffered from an entrenched orthodoxy: rows of text-heavy display boards, passive brochures, and minimal audience engagement. When communicating constitutional frameworks such as the Constitution and the Basic Law, the industry default has habitually leaned toward solemn lectures rather than experiential discovery. Firebean boldly challenged this obsolete paradigm. Tasked by the Constitutional and Mainland Affairs Bureau, our team reconceptualised public education from the ground up, discarding didactic posters in favour of a futuristic 'Robotics Training Academy' deployed within prime shopping retail atriums.

The strategic breakthrough lay in establishing an emotional and sensory bridge before introducing legal principles. At the core of the activation was the original intellectual property 'Dr. Owl'—a scholarly mentor reimagined within a bespoke mechanical exoskeleton cockpit. By engineering a tangible 1:1 sci-fi icon as the central anchor, the pavilion immediately disrupted normal mall foot traffic. Shoppers of all demographics were not merely summoned to absorb statutory articles; they were invited into an imaginative narrative universe where constitutional literacy functioned as the operational fuel for robotic mastery.

To solve the challenge of substantive educational retention, Firebean engineered custom interactive installations that merged high-spec digital UI/UX with intuitive mechanics. The pavilion featured custom drone simulators, immersive curved-screen arcade flight cockpits, and mecha maze consoles. Rather than presenting statutory texts as static obstacles, the game mechanics seamlessly embedded constitutional milestones directly into task-oriented challenges. Participants absorbed constitutional tenets as actionable knowledge required to advance through each mission, shifting their role from passive recipients to empowered problem-solvers.

Managing spatial throughput and emotional satisfaction in high-density shopping venues demands rigorous engineering and crowd-flow architecture. Firebean orchestrated a radial layout with multi-station central touch-consoles and perimeter flight modules, effectively dispersing attendee density. The journey concluded at a custom-engineered claw machine zone, transforming conventional gift hand-outs into an interactive milestone. By claiming proprietary Dr. Owl merchandise through their own gameplay, visitors completed an empowering, high-recall brand cycle that unified parent-child collaboration with civic literacy.

True public relations disruption occurs when statutory gravity is not softened, but elevated through uncompromising experiential storytelling that turns civic responsibility into genuine cultural delight.

繁體中文

顛覆政宣策劃：當莊嚴法規蛻變為機甲科幻體驗

Firebean 打破單向灌輸的傳統展板思維，以高規格沉浸式機械學院重構《憲法》與《基本法》的公眾傳播維度。

長期以來，公營機構的法制與政策宣傳往往受困於陳舊的既定框架：密密麻麻的文字展板、單向派發的印刷單張，以及流於形式的被動接觸。面對《憲法》與《基本法》這類講求準確性與法理權威的嚴肅課題，業界普遍採取穩妥卻成效低落的教條式陳列。Firebean 堅決顛覆這種傳統慣性。在受政制及內地事務局委託之際，團隊大膽打破常規，摒棄傳統政宣展板的刻板面貌，將各大旗艦商場中庭轉化為極具未來質感的「機械訓練學院」。

這場公關革新的戰略核心，在於以頂尖的敘事包裝化解條文距離感。Firebean 精心孵化出全方位原創 IP「Dr. Owl 貓頭鷹博士」，讓其身披科技機甲戰甲坐鎮展區中央。1:1 實體機甲座艙裝置結合冷光藍與金屬灰的俐落工藝，在熙來攘往的商業中庭中確立了強烈的視覺焦點。這種具備跨世代吸引力的科幻美學，成功打破傳統公營項目的枯燥觀感，將過路公眾由被動的旁觀者，迅速轉化為懷抱好奇心踏入學院的積極探索者。

要實現實質的法治知識轉化，關鍵在於將嚴謹內容無縫植入遊戲邏輯。Firebean 團隊主導了全套沉浸式互動裝置的軟硬件研發，涵蓋模擬無人機駕駛、超寬曲面屏幕街機駕駛艙，以及機甲迷宮操縱台。條文考核不再是生硬的問答環節，而是成為解鎖各項飛行與操控任務的核心密碼。參觀者在全神貫注操控搖桿的沉浸體驗中，自然理解條文脈絡與法治精神，達致真正意義上的寓教於樂。

在商場開放環境中處理龐大人流，團隊以精密的動線規劃建立了完整的體驗閉環。展位採用中央環形操作台配合外圍駕駛艙的放射式佈局，有效分散人流擁堵點；動線終點則設置高人氣的訂製「夾娃娃機」獎勵區。參觀者憑闖關成果親手夾取專屬毛公仔周邊，將學習成效轉化為具體的情感獎勵。這項設計不僅引發跨年齡層的自發參與，更在親子互動間促成法治觀念的深度紮根。

卓越的公共關係重塑不在於淡化議題的嚴肅性，而是以頂尖的體驗設計賦予硬核知識溫度，讓公民意識在沉浸互動中自然生長。
