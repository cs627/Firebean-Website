Beyond the Plate: Unveiling the Culinary Innovation Lab at Outback Steakhouse

Firebean secures exclusive access for media to Bloomin’ Brands’ R&D Kitchen Table, offering a rare glimpse into the artistry and science of global menu development.

In an industry often perceived through the lens of plated perfection, the true alchemy of culinary innovation frequently remains a closely guarded secret. Firebean was privileged to pull back this curtain, orchestrating an exclusive “R&D Kitchen Table: Media Chat & Happy Hour” with Bloomin’ Brands’ Vice President of R&D and Corporate Executive Chef, Efrem Cutler, at CulinArt 1862. This event was not merely a tasting; it was an intimate, behind-the-scenes masterclass, designed to transform perceptions of menu development from a routine task into a sophisticated fusion of art and science.

The challenge for a global brand like Outback Steakhouse lies in consistently delivering distinctive, high-quality dining experiences while navigating diverse palates and operational complexities across international markets. Many brands struggle to articulate the rigorous, iterative process that translates a chef's inspiration into a beloved menu item. Our strategy was to leverage Chef Cutler’s 45 years of experience and his role in shaping Outback’s global culinary identity, presenting him not just as a chef, but as a visionary architect of flavour. By inviting key media and KOLs into this ‘kitchen table’ setting, we provided an unprecedented opportunity to engage directly with the mind behind the innovation.

The event structure itself was a departure from conventional media briefings. Instead of a formal presentation, guests enjoyed selected dishes and fine wines, fostering a relaxed yet intellectually stimulating environment. Chef Cutler offered candid insights into the intricate balance of flavour conception, ingredient sourcing, market trends, and practical restaurant execution. This candid sharing, facilitated by Firebean's meticulous event design and curated guest list, allowed attendees to witness the journey of a dish from abstract concept to tangible table presence, demystifying the R&D process and highlighting the deep expertise underpinning every Outback creation.

Moreover, featuring Chef Cutler’s appearance on MasterChef Brasil Season 9 as an Outback expert chef further amplified his authority and charisma, providing a compelling narrative for media. This reinforced his status as an international culinary personality and underscored Bloomin’ Brands’ commitment to culinary excellence on a global stage. The meticulous curation of visuals, from the chef’s precise plating to candid interactions, ensured that every published piece would convey the professionalism, creativity, and approachable expertise central to this exclusive experience.

Ultimately, by turning the often-opaque process of culinary R&D into a transparent, engaging, and exclusive media experience, Firebean successfully positioned Outback Steakhouse and Bloomin’ Brands as pioneers of gastronomic innovation and thought leaders in the global restaurant industry.

超越餐桌：揭秘 Outback Steakhouse 廚藝創新背後的研發實驗室

Firebean 成功為傳媒開啟 Bloomin’ Brands 研發廚房之門，獨家窺探全球餐單開發的藝術與科學。

在一個常被視為追求餐盤完美呈現的行業中，廚藝創新的真正奧秘往往是深藏不露的秘密。Firebean 有幸揭開這層面紗，在 CulinArt 1862 為 Bloomin’ Brands 研發副總裁兼集團行政總廚 Efrem Cutler 策劃了一場獨家的「R&D Kitchen Table: Media Chat & Happy Hour」活動。這不僅僅是一場品鑑會；它更是一場親密而深入的幕後大師班，旨在將餐單開發從例行公事轉變為藝術與科學的精妙結合。

像 Outback Steakhouse 這樣的全球品牌，其挑戰在於如何在迎合多元口味和國際市場複雜營運的同時，持續提供獨特而高品質的餐飲體驗。許多品牌難以清晰闡述將主廚靈感轉化為受歡迎菜式的嚴謹迭代過程。我們的策略是善用 Chef Cutler 45 年的豐富經驗及其在塑造 Outback 全球烹飪形象方面的作用，將他呈現為一位風味的願景建築師，而不僅僅是一名廚師。透過邀請主要媒體和 KOL 參與這種「廚房餐桌」式的設定，我們提供了前所未有的機會，讓他們直接與創新背後的主導者進行交流。

活動本身的結構也擺脫了傳統媒體簡報會的形式。來賓一邊享用精選菜餚和美酒，一邊營造出輕鬆卻又富含啟發性的交流氛圍。Chef Cutler 坦率地分享了風味構思、食材採購、市場趨勢以及實際餐廳營運之間複雜的平衡過程。Firebean 精心策劃的活動設計和嘉賓名單，促成了這場坦誠的分享，讓與會者親眼見證一道菜從抽象概念到具體上桌的旅程，揭開了研發過程的神秘面紗，凸顯了 Outback 每道菜式背後深厚的專業知識。

此外，Chef Cutler 曾以 Outback 專家主廚身份亮相《MasterChef Brasil》第九季，這一點進一步提升了他的權威性和魅力，為媒體提供了引人入勝的敘事素材。這強化了他作為國際烹飪權威的地位，並強調了 Bloomin’ Brands 在全球舞台上對卓越烹飪的承諾。從主廚精準的擺盤到真誠的互動，精心策劃的視覺內容確保了每一篇報導都能傳達專業性、創造力以及這次獨家體驗的核心 — 親和而專業的專家形象。

最終，透過將原本晦澀難懂的餐飲研發過程轉化為一場透明、引人入勝且獨特的媒體體驗，Firebean 成功將 Outback Steakhouse 及 Bloomin’ Brands 定位為美食創新先驅和全球餐飲業界的思想領袖。
