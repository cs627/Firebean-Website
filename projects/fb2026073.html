Gamifying Safety: How Firebean Engaged Millions for the Buildings Department's 2023 Campaign

The Challenge: Transforming Public Perception of Building Safety

The Buildings Department faced a perennial challenge: how to effectively disseminate crucial building safety information to the general public in an engaging and memorable way. Traditional public awareness campaigns often struggle with cut-through, particularly among younger demographics, when dealing with complex, serious topics. For their 2023 large-scale promotional plan, the client needed an innovative approach that would not only educate but also actively involve citizens, drive online interaction, and translate that digital engagement into physical attendance at key events. The goal was to make building safety relevant, accessible, and even fun.

Firebean's Strategic Intervention: A Gamified Approach to Education

Firebean recognized that to overcome public apathy, a radical shift from conventional methods was required. Our analytical problem-solving approach led us to a groundbreaking solution: gamification. We conceptualized and developed an interactive mobile game – 'Catch the Dangerous Building Spirits' – which transformed abstract building safety concepts into an immersive and exciting digital quest. This strategic pivot allowed us to distill complex information into an easily digestible, interactive format, significantly enhancing public interest and retention. Firebean’s comprehensive scope of work, including Concept Development, Branding Strategy, PR Consulting, Theme Design, and Visual Identity, ensured a cohesive and impactful campaign from the ground up.

Executing a Multi-Channel, Interactive Campaign

With the game as its core, Firebean designed a multi-channel promotional blitz to ensure maximum reach. We deployed eye-catching Key Visual (KV) designs across high-visibility platforms, including bus, tram, and digital panel advertisements, strategically guiding citizens to the game. Our UI/UX Design expertise ensured the game was intuitive and highly engaging, while bespoke Video Production and Motion Graphics amplified its appeal across social media and other digital channels. To further drive real-world engagement, Firebean implemented a clever online-to-offline incentive: exclusive in-game 'boss' spirits were strategically released at the physical event location, alongside the offer of convenience store cash vouchers for collected spirits. This powerful mechanism dramatically boosted event footfall and participation, effectively bridging the digital and physical worlds.

Measurable Impact and Community Engagement

The success of the initial game launch laid the groundwork for the second phase: promoting 'Building Safety Week.' Firebean continued to leverage diverse channels, disseminating essential information and seminar schedules with a refreshed, vibrant visual identity and compelling Social Media Content. Our Technical Support ensured seamless execution of the interactive installations and overall digital infrastructure. The campaign not only educated a vast number of citizens on crucial building safety knowledge but also fostered a sense of community participation. By making safety learning an enjoyable and rewarding experience, Firebean successfully transformed a societal obligation into an active civic engagement.

Firebean's innovative gamified strategy didn't just promote building safety; it redefined how public awareness campaigns can achieve unparalleled engagement and measurable impact.

破格創新：Firebean 如何透過遊戲化策略為屋宇署2023宣傳計劃締造非凡成果

挑戰重重：重塑大眾對樓宇安全的認知

屋宇署一直面對一個挑戰：如何有效地以具吸引力且令人難忘的方式，向廣大市民傳達關鍵的樓宇安全資訊。傳統的公眾宣傳活動，尤其在處理複雜嚴肅的議題時，往往難以吸引公眾注意，特別是年輕一代。對於2023年的大型宣傳計劃，客戶需要一種創新的方法，不僅能教育市民，更能積極鼓勵他們參與，推動線上互動，並將這些數碼參與轉化為實體活動的出席率。目標是要讓樓宇安全變得相關、易於接觸，甚至充滿樂趣。

Firebean 的策略性介入：以遊戲化提升安全教育成效

Firebean 意識到，要克服公眾的冷漠，必須徹底改變傳統方法。我們以分析問題解決者的角度出發，提出了一個突破性的解決方案：遊戲化。我們構思並開發了一款互動手機遊戲——「捉危樓精靈」，將抽象的樓宇安全概念轉化為一個沉浸式且刺激的數碼任務。這種策略性轉變使我們能夠將複雜的資訊濃縮成易於理解的互動形式，顯著提升了公眾的興趣和資訊保留度。Firebean 提供的全面服務範疇，包括概念開發、品牌策略、公關顧問、主題設計和視覺識別，確保了整個宣傳計劃從基礎開始便具備凝聚力和影響力。

精準執行：多渠道互動宣傳攻勢

以遊戲為核心，Firebean 設計了一場多渠道的宣傳攻勢，以確保最大程度的覆蓋率。我們利用吸睛的關鍵視覺（KV）設計，投放於高曝光率平台，包括巴士、電車和數碼廣告牌，策略性地引導市民參與遊戲。我們的 UI/UX 設計專業知識確保遊戲直觀且高度吸引人，而定制的影片製作和動態圖像則透過社交媒體及其他數碼渠道放大其魅力。為了進一步推動實體參與，Firebean 實施了一個巧妙的線上線下獎勵機制：獨家遊戲內「大佬精靈」被策略性地安排在實體活動現場發放，同時提供收集足夠精靈換取便利店現金券的獎勵。這個強大的機制顯著提高了活動的人流和參與度，成功地連接了數碼世界與實體體驗。

成果豐碩：引領公眾參與及深度互動

遊戲初期成功的推出為第二階段——推廣「樓宇安全週」奠定了基礎。Firebean 繼續利用多元化的渠道，以全新的、充滿活力的視覺識別和引人入勝的社交媒體內容，傳播重要的資訊和講座日程。我們的技術支援確保了互動裝置和整體數碼基礎設施的無縫運作。這次宣傳活動不僅向大量市民普及了關鍵的樓宇安全知識，更培養了社區參與感。通過讓安全學習成為一個愉悅且有回報的體驗，Firebean 成功地將一項社會義務轉化為積極的公民參與。

Firebean 創新的遊戲化策略不僅宣傳了樓宇安全，更重新定義了公眾宣傳活動如何實現無與倫比的參與度和可衡量的影響。
