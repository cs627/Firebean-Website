PR Agency in Hong Kong — government, lifestyle and F&B public relations

Firebean is a Hong Kong public relations agency that plans and runs communications campaigns for government bureaus, lifestyle and F&B brands — media relations, public engagement, content and events, delivered by one team from Wan Chai since 2007.

What we do

  • Media relations & press office — announcements, briefings, interviews and crisis-ready messaging in English, 繁體中文 and 日本語.
  • Public engagement campaigns — consultation support, community outreach and exhibition-led education for policy topics.
  • Content & social — campaign copy, video, motion and always-on social for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Threads, Xiaohongshu and YouTube.
  • Issues and stakeholder mapping — audience research, message testing and reporting that stakeholders actually read.
  • Event-led PR — launches, ceremonies and roving exhibitions where the media moment and the public experience are the same project.

Frequently asked questions

What does a Hong Kong PR agency do for a government client?
It plans and runs public communication for a policy or service: messaging, media handling, stakeholder and community engagement, often paired with exhibitions or events so the public meets the content in person. Firebean has delivered this for bureaus including Constitutional and Mainland Affairs, Education, Security Bureau (Narcotics Division), Civil Service, Buildings Department and Development Bureau.
Do you work in English, Cantonese and Japanese?
Yes. Campaign copy, media materials and on-site hosting are produced in English, Traditional Chinese and Japanese, and government work is routinely produced bilingually.
How is PR priced?
By scope: a retained engagement, a single campaign, or a project that combines communications with an exhibition or event. We quote after understanding the audience, the timeline and the deliverables — never from a menu.
How quickly can a campaign start?
A media announcement can move within days; a public engagement campaign with an exhibition build typically needs four to eight weeks depending on approvals and venue availability.
Can you support a public consultation?
Yes — messaging, stakeholder mapping, community sessions and the reporting that shows what was heard.
How do you prove results?
Media coverage and sentiment, reach and engagement of owned channels, participation data from events and exhibitions, and search/AI visibility. Every report states what was measured and what was not.
