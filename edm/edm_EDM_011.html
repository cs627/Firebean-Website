SECTION 02 — 市場洞察 / MARKET INSIGHT
Transparency & Trust

Transparency & Accountability in Government PR

Only 17% of Americans trust their government — transparency is the fastest path back to public confidence.

Key Stat: 17%

Only 17% of Americans say they trust the federal government to do what is right (Pew Research, 2025). Globally, trust in public institutions has been declining for over a decade. The OECD's 2026 Trust Survey found that perceived integrity — including transparency and accountability — is the single strongest driver of trust in government.

Key Stat: 74%

74% of citizens say transparency about how public funds are used positively affects their trust (Independent Sector, 2026). 73% say the same about knowing where funding comes from. For government communicators, this means every event, exhibition, and campaign should include visible reporting mechanisms — budget breakdowns, attendee data, and outcome metrics.

Key Stat: 3x

Government agencies that proactively publish open data and performance metrics experience 3x higher citizen satisfaction scores (OECD, 2026). Transparency is not just ethical — it is operationally effective. Citizens who understand how decisions are made are more forgiving when outcomes fall short and more supportive when new initiatives are proposed. Hong Kong has a strong foundation: the Digital Policy Office's open data portal, the Smart Government Innovation Lab, and a tradition of public consultation. The next step is embedding transparency into the DNA of every public engagement touchpoint — from event registration to post-campaign reporting.
