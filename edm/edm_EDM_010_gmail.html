SECTION 02 — 市場洞察 / MARKET INSIGHT
Community Planning

Community-Led Event Planning: Co-Creating the Civic Experience

Citizens who participate in planning are 3x more likely to support the final outcome — even when it differs from their preference.

Participation Builds Support

Citizens who participate in community planning processes are 3x more likely to support the final outcome — even when it differs from their preference (OECD, 2026). The act of being heard matters more than getting one's way. This finding has profound implications for government PR: process transparency is more powerful than outcome marketing.

The Participation Gap

67% of citizens say they want more opportunities to participate in local decision-making, but only 22% feel they currently have meaningful input (OECD Trust Survey, 2026). The gap between desire and opportunity represents a massive untapped reservoir of public goodwill.

Co-Creation Cuts Opposition

Community co-creation reduces project opposition by 40% and accelerates approval timelines by an average of 3-4 months (UK Ministry of Housing, 2025). Early engagement is cheaper than late-stage conflict. For Hong Kong's district councils and development bureaux, the lesson is clear: invest in genuine co-creation workshops, not token consultation sessions. Hong Kong's 18 districts each have distinct character and priorities. A one-size-fits-all public engagement approach misses the point. The departments that embrace hyperlocal, community-led planning — facilitated by professional facilitators and supported by digital feedback platforms — will build the trust that makes difficult decisions possible.
