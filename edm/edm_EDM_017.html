SECTION 02 — 市場洞察 / MARKET INSIGHT
Behavioural Design

Strategic Nudges: Small Prompts, Measurable Behaviour

The best nudge is not a louder message — it is a better-designed choice at the moment of decision. That one line separates two entirely different ways of changing behaviour. The loud way keeps adding pressure: more reminders, more urgency, more noise, all of it trying to change what people want. The designed way changes the decision itself — the form placed in their hand, the default already ticked, the sign standing exactly where they hesitate, the object given at the second it is needed. Nothing is demanded of anyone's willpower, and nothing has to be taken away from the experience. The right choice simply becomes easier to make than to avoid. Which is why the numbers on this page are not a story about persuasion. They are a story about design.

Public bodies using behavioural science, OECD 2023

Governments stopped treating this as a communication trick a while ago. The OECD now tracks behavioural science teams sitting inside public bodies worldwide, because simply telling people what to do stopped working long before anyone admitted it.

Extra vaccinations from one reworded SMS, BIT 2025

The evidence for restraint is just as useful. When the UK Behavioural Insights Team ran nationwide randomised trials on 4 million adults, the winning text was not the most urgent or the most informative one - it was the message that simply told people they had reached the top of the queue. One rewording, tens of thousands of additional vaccinations.

Average at-scale nudge effect, NBER 2020

And the honest number: across 126 randomised trials covering 23 million people, nudges delivered at government scale moved behaviour by about 1.4 percentage points - roughly one sixth of the effect reported in academic papers. Real, repeatable, modest. Which is exactly why the physical layer still matters: a prompt on paper, a sign on the floor or a souvenir in someone's hand does not compete with the campaign, it finishes the job. Firebean designs that layer, so the behaviour you need becomes the easiest thing to do next.
