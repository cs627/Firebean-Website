Data-Driven PR

Data-Driven Public Sentiment Analysis for Gov PR

Turn event foot traffic into actionable public sentiment data.

Traditional 'head-counting' at events is being replaced by sophisticated sentiment analysis. By using anonymous heat-mapping and interactive digital polls, government departments can now measure not just how many people attended, but how they felt about the policy message. This data is invaluable for justifying marketing spend and refining future communications to better serve the public's needs and concerns.
