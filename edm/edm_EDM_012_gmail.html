SECTION 02 — 市場洞察 / MARKET INSIGHT
Micro-Influencer Strategy

The Rise of Micro-Influencers in Local Gov Comms

Micro-influencers deliver 3.2x higher engagement at 60% lower cost than macro-influencers — and government should take note.

Smaller Audience, Higher Trust

Micro-influencers (10K-100K followers) average 7.2% engagement rates versus 1.7% for macro-influencers (Scoop Market, 2026). On Instagram specifically, micro-creators achieve 3.86% engagement against 1.21% for mega-influencers. 82% of consumers are highly likely to follow recommendations made by micro-influencers.

Micro Wins on ROI

Micro-influencer campaigns cost 60% less per post than macro-influencer campaigns while delivering 2.4x higher conversion rates. The cost-per-engagement is $0.18 for micro versus $1.67 for mega-influencers (Digital Applied, 2026). Ten micro-influencers typically outperform one macro-influencer on combined reach, audience diversity, and conversion.

The Effective Majority

78% of successful influencer campaigns in 2026 involve micro-influencers. 89% of brands plan to maintain or increase micro-influencer investment. For government departments, this suggests a powerful alternative to expensive celebrity endorsements: mobilise local community voices — district councillors, neighbourhood leaders, parent bloggers — who already have the trust and attention of target audiences. Hong Kong's tight-knit district communities are ideally suited to micro-influencer strategies. A Tsuen Wan parent blogger carries more weight with Tsuen Wan families than a TVB celebrity ever could — and costs a fraction of the budget.
