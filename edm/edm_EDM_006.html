Ancient Egypt Unveiled: 250 Treasures Closing 31 August

The largest and most comprehensive ancient Egyptian exhibition ever staged in Hong Kong enters its final weeks. Jointly organised by the Hong Kong Palace Museum and Egypt’s Supreme Council of Antiquities, ‘Ancient Egypt Unveiled’ presents 250 rare artefacts — colossal pharaoh statues, gold jewellery, mummy coffins and animal mummies — drawn from seven Egyptian museums and the Saqqara archaeological site. Don’t miss the last chance to experience nearly 5,000 years of Egyptian civilisation in the West Kowloon Cultural District.

SECTION 02 — 市場洞察 / MARKET INSIGHT
Hybrid Event Strategies

Hybrid Events: Bridging the Digital Divide in Civic Outreach

Combine the impact of physical events with the reach of digital platforms.

The 'Phygital' (Physical + Digital) model is the new standard for civic engagement. While a physical roving exhibition builds local community trust, a digital twin allows for city-wide deliberation. In 2026, the most successful government campaigns are those that use physical touchpoints to drive traffic to digital platforms, ensuring that even those who cannot attend in person can still participate in the conversation and access vital public information.
