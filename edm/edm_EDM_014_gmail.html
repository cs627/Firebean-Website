SECTION 02 — 市場洞察 / MARKET INSIGHT
Mascot-Led Engagement

Interactive Mascot-Led Engagement: Humanizing the Department

Brand mascots boost recall to 88% and supercharge engagement — why should government departments be any different?

Key Stat: 88%

Campaigns using brand mascots or recurring characters achieve 88% brand recall, compared to significantly lower rates for logo-only or celebrity-led campaigns (System1, 2025). Characters score 105 on the Brand Asset Tracking scale versus 82 for logos and 31 for fonts.

Key Stat: 73%

Campaigns with recurring brand devices like mascots are 73% more likely to generate significant profit increases (IPA Database). Mascots build emotional connection over time — every appearance compounds familiarity and trust. Most brands see statistically meaningful shifts in recall and sentiment within 3-6 months of consistent mascot deployment.

Key Stat: 3.8

Ads featuring brand characters scored an average of 3.8 Stars on emotional engagement metrics, versus 2.7 Stars for celebrity-led ads (System1 Super Bowl study). Mascots also delivered stronger short-term sales impact. The lesson for government: a well-designed departmental mascot — like the Hong Kong Police's "Safety Bear" or FEHD's "Keep Clean Ambassador" — outperforms a celebrity spokesperson on engagement and cost-efficiency. Hong Kong already has a tradition of government mascots. The opportunity is to deploy them more strategically: across digital and physical touchpoints, in consistent brand voice, with merchandise and social media presence that turns a one-off campaign into a lasting public relationship.
 
SECTION 03 — 成功案例 / CASE STUDY
GOVERNMENTBuildings Department (屋宇署)

Transforming Public Engagement: Firebean's Strategic Social Media for the Buildings Department

How Firebean turned complex building safety regulations into viral social media content with mascots, trending topics, and interactive campaigns.

The Buildings Department (BD) faced a unique challenge: how to transform seemingly 'dry' regulatory information into content that not only educates but genuinely engages the public. Firebean implemented a multi-pronged strategy: developing diverse content categories, leveraging a consistent and relatable mascot, and strategically integrating trending topics with core building safety messages. Creative initiatives included the 'Mascot Live-Action Series' where the mascot 'inspected' sites, a Valentine's Day series linking relationships to building safety, and pre-prepared weather advisory image sets for quick deployment during typhoons. Through monthly review cycles and data-driven adaptation, the BD's social media transformed from static information to a vibrant, educational, and highly engaging platform — setting a new benchmark for public sector communication.
